# Shared upload player

All Guitar Pro uploads generated by `analyzer/build_tab_from_gp5.py` reference `tabs/generated-alphatab-player.js`. The shared player is the single standard behavior for current and future uploaded tabs. Instrument switching must preserve the global playback position instead of resetting to bar 1.

## Administration flow

New uploads do not enter the public catalog immediately.

1. `tab-upload.html` sends Guitar Pro and the YouTube URL to the upload Worker.
2. `build-tab-music.yml` creates the player, data file, and `drafts/<slug>.json` manifest.
3. The admin previews the production player and calibrates the YouTube offset.
4. **Terbitkan ke katalog** calls the Worker's `/publish` endpoint.
5. `publish-tab-music.yml` saves the final offset, adds the entry to `tab-catalog.json`, and removes the draft manifest.

While a tab is a draft, its direct preview URL works but the song is absent from the Tab Musik catalog.
